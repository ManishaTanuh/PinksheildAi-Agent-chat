# PinkShieldAI

Agentic AI platform for insurance companies — transforming breast cancer risk assessment into an intelligent, multilingual chatbot that optimizes questionnaire flow based on risk prevalence data.

## Vision

Insurance companies need better tools to assess cancer risk during underwriting and wellness programs. PinkShieldAI provides:

- **Adaptive questionnaire engine** — dynamically adjusts questions based on prior answers and population-level risk data
- **Multilingual voice + chat** — reaches policyholders in their preferred Indian language
- **Risk stratification** — flags high-risk individuals for early intervention, reducing claims cost
- **Insurer dashboard** — analytics on risk prevalence, questionnaire completion rates, and portfolio health
