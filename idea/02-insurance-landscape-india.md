# Insurance Landscape — India

## Market Overview

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total general insurance premium (FY2024) | ~₹2.90 lakh crore (~$35B) |
| Health insurance GWP (FY2024) | ~₹1.0–1.1 lakh crore (~$12–13B) |
| Health insurance CAGR (5-year) | 15–20% |
| Insurance penetration (overall) | ~3.7–4% of GDP |
| General insurance penetration | ~1% of GDP (global avg: 3.5%) |

| Projected health insurance market (FY2027–28) | ₹1.5–1.8 lakh crore |
| Medical inflation (annual) | 10–14% |

### Growth Drivers
- Rising healthcare costs
- Post-COVID health awareness (retail health premium growing 20%+ annually)
- IRDAI regulatory push for inclusion
- Tax benefits under Section 80D
- Rising chronic disease burden (diabetes, cancer, CVD)
- Growing middle class

## Regulatory Body — IRDAI

The **Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI)** is the apex regulator for all insurance in India.

### Key Regulations
- **IRDAI (Health Insurance) Regulations 2016** — foundational framework
- **Standardized exclusions (2020)** — uniform exclusion list across all health policies
- **Portability rules** — switch insurers without losing waiting period credit
- **Mandatory coverage** — mental health, HIV/AIDS treatment now required

### Recent Reforms (2023–2025)
| Reform | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Bima Sugam** | Digital insurance marketplace ("UPI of insurance") — policy purchase, claims, portability |
| **Bima Vistaar** | Low-cost bundled insurance (life + health + property + PA) at ~₹1,500/year |
| **Bima Vahak** | Women-centric distribution for rural/underserved areas |
| **Use and File** | Insurers can launch products first, file with IRDAI later — faster time-to-market |
| **Agent deregulation** | Agents can now sell products from multiple insurers (was 1:1) |
| **Cashless Everywhere (2024)** | All network hospitals must provide cashless settlement |
| **Pre-existing disease** | Max waiting period reduced to 3 years (from 4) |
| **IRDAI Sandbox** | 150+ insurtech proposals received; enables innovation in controlled environment |
| **ABHA integration** | Health data portability via Ayushman Bharat Health Account |
